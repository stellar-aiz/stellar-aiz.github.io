Agents

Three AI agents that
pass the work along.

To-dos from a meeting go into the ledger, get worked on, and the results feed the next meeting's decisions. Three agents run the basic cycle of work, deciding, planning and doing, like a relay.

  1. 1
    DECIDE ／ ACT

    Shepherd

    Sits in on meetings and speaks up only when it adds value. Afterwards it writes up the minutes and logs to-dos and issues.

  2. 2
    PLAN & CHECK

    Doberman

    Merges to-dos into the ledger and resolves duplicates and conflicts. It flags overdue or unassigned items and keeps the project context current.

  3. 3
    DO

    Yorkie

    Picks up a task from the ledger and switches skills to research, analyze and draft. Its deliverables go back into the next meeting.
